About BookDiscovery

BookDiscovery is a book discovery website designed to help readers find books that match their interests, goals, and reading preferences. Instead of searching through countless books, users can explore relevant books through different discovery features.

🔎 Smart Book Search

Search for books using a title, author, or keyword and quickly explore the available results.

🎯 Goal-Based Discovery

Choose a personal goal such as improving study habits, communication, finance, or personal growth and discover books related to it.

⚡ Quick Pick

Select your goal, reading style, and reading experience to receive a personalized selection of books.

📚 Explore Categories

Browse books across categories such as psychology, business, finance, education, biography, science, creativity, and more.

❤️ Save Favorites

Registered users can save interesting books to their favorites and access them whenever they want.

📖 Detailed Information

View important book information including the cover, author, description, publisher, pages, rating, and publication details.
